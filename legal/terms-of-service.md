>
> So this document deliberately **does not** impose obligations on Muse users beyond Meta's own terms. ## 1. What Porter does

Porter connects your AI assistant to local service businesses through the booking systems those businesses already use. You ask for something; Porter finds options, books them, and can take payment.

**Porter is not the service provider.** We do not cut hair, fix pipes or clean houses. The business you choose does the work, and your agreement for that work is with them.

## 2. What you can expect from us

- We show you prices, times and cancellation terms **before** anything is booked or charged.
- We re-check that an appointment time is still free immediately before booking it, and tell you clearly if it has gone.
- We take payment only for an amount you have confirmed.
- We pass on only the details a business needs to do the job.

## 3. What we ask of you

- Give accurate contact and address details, so the business can reach you and turn up at the right place.
- Turn up, or cancel in time. Cancellation fees are set by the business, not by us, and we show them to you before you commit.
- Do not use Porter to arrange anything unlawful.

## 4. Bookings

A booking is a request until the business confirms it. Some platforms confirm immediately; others, particularly home-service trades, schedule the work afterwards. We tell you which state your booking is in and never describe a request as confirmed.

Prices and availability come from the business. We show them as we receive them. If something changes between our showing it and your confirming it, the confirmation stops and we ask you again.

## 5. Payments and cancellations

Payments are handled by our payment processor. We never see or store your card details.

Cancellation terms are the business's own. We show them before you book and again before you cancel. Where a business publishes no cancellation policy, we say so rather than implying that cancelling is free.

If you cancel and a fee applies, we collect it. If that collection fails, your cancellation still stands and the fee remains owed.

## 6. If something goes wrong

Tell us. Contact details are at the bottom. We will help you sort it out with the business, and we will correct anything on our side.

For problems with the work itself — a bad haircut, a leak that was not fixed — your claim is with the business that did it. We will give you what we hold about the booking to support you.

## 7. Availability

We try to keep Porter working and accurate. We cannot promise that every business's system is always reachable, or that their published availability is always right. When a platform is down, we say so instead of showing you stale information.

## 8. Ending your use

You can disconnect Porter at any time in your assistant's settings. We may suspend access if Porter is being used unlawfully or in a way that harms a business or another user.

On request we delete your data, as described in the [Privacy Notice](./privacy-notice.md).

## 9. Liability

Nothing here limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that cannot be limited by law.
