## What Porter is

Porter connects an AI assistant to the booking systems that local businesses already use, so you can find, book, reschedule, cancel and pay for services by asking. We are not the business that does the work. We pass your request to them.

## What we collect

**Only what a booking needs.** Different businesses need different things, and we send each one only what it actually requires.

| Data | Why | Who receives it |
|---|---|---|
| Your first name | So the business knows who is coming | The business, via its booking platform |
| Contact detail (email or phone) | So the business can reach you about the appointment | The business |
| Service address | Only for work at your home, such as plumbing or cleaning | Only businesses that travel to you |
| What you asked for | To find the right service and time | Used by Porter; the job description goes to the business for quoted work |
| Booking records | So you can see and change your appointments | Held by Porter and by the business |
| Payment references | To take a deposit or payment | Our payment processor |

**We never collect or store card numbers.** You save a payment method with our payment processor directly. Porter holds only an opaque reference to it.

**We do not build a profile of you.** Your booking history answers "what have I got coming up" and nothing else. We do not use it to infer anything about your health, beliefs, or any other personal characteristic, and we do not use it to target you.

## What we send onwards

We pass your details to the business you chose, and to the booking platform that business runs, because that is the only way to make the appointment. We send the minimum each one needs.

**No recipient gets broader access, purpose, or retention rights than we hold ourselves.** A business receives your details to carry out your booking, not for anything else.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it for advertising.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as it takes to fulfil your request and to keep a record of the booking.

**When you disconnect Porter or ask us to delete your data, we delete it.** That covers your bookings, your payment records and your saved payment reference.

We keep an operational log of what actions were taken — which tool ran, when, whether it worked. **That log contains no personal data**: it holds hashes and outcomes, not names, emails, phone numbers or addresses. It survives a deletion request because there is nothing personal in it to delete, and it is what lets us answer questions about what happened.

## Your choices

- **See your activity.** Ask your assistant, or request your audit trail from us.
- **Disconnect at any time.** Muse lets you disconnect a connector in its settings. That stops any further exchange of data.
- **Ask us to delete your data.** Write to the address below.
- **Other rights.** Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, port or object to the processing of your data. Write to us and we will help.

Note that once a business has your booking details, that business holds them under its own privacy policy. Disconnecting Porter does not erase their copy. Contact the business directly for that.

## Security

Credentials for booking platforms are encrypted at rest and decrypted only for the duration of a single request. Your data is encrypted under a key that belongs to you alone, so destroying that key makes any remaining copy unreadable.

If a security incident affects your data, we notify Meta within 48 hours and will tell you where the law requires it.
